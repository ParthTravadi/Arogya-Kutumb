# Arogya-Kutumb
Holistic Family Health · Nutrition · Ayurveda

Everything runs in your browser - zero login, zero data shared.


🗺 How to use Arogya Kutumb

👨‍👩‍👧
1 - Family Setup
Add each member's name, age, weight and Prakriti. The app personalises everything around them.

📍
2 - Set Location
Tap Live detect for your city and live AQI. Used for season-specific nutrition tips.

🫓
3 - Roti Calculator
Your family's seasonal roti recipe: flour ratios, ghee, spices, calculated for your Prakriti.

✅
4 - Daily Tracker
Check off daily Ayurvedic habits: water, walk, sleep, supplements. Builds a streak.

🌿
5 - Seasonal Guide
Ritu-specific dos and don'ts: foods, timing, Ayurvedic herbs for your current season.

⭐
6 - Rashifal
Daily Vedic reading for your Rashi: health tip, auspicious time, Sanskrit phrase.
Start with Family Setup, tap Live detect for location, then go to Dashboard for your AI daily tip.
