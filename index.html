📿 How to chant
1. Sit facing East or North — morning is ideal.
2. Light a ghee diya or incense if available.
3. Take 3 deep breaths before starting.
4. Chant 108 times (1 mala) or minimum 11 times.
5. Sit in silence for 2 minutes after completing.
🕰 Best times
Brahma Muhurta — 4:00–6:00 AM (most powerful)
Sunrise — 6:00–7:00 AM (ideal before roti prep)
Sandhya — sunset 6:00–7:00 PM
Before sleep — calms Vata, builds Ojas overnight
